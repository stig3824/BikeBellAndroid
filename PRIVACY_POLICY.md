# Privacy Policy for BikeBell

**Last updated:** January 2025

## Overview

BikeBell is a simple Android application that turns your device into a virtual bicycle bell. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

BikeBell operates entirely on your device and does not:
- Collect personal information
- Store data on external servers
- Share data with third parties
- Track your usage
- Send analytics or crash reports

## Permissions Used

BikeBell requests the following permissions:

### Required Permissions
- **VIBRATE**: To provide haptic feedback when the bell rings
- **MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS**: To control audio output and speaker mode
- **WAKE_LOCK**: To keep the screen on during use

### Hardware Features
- **Accelerometer**: To detect device motion for bell activation
- **Audio Output**: To play bell sounds

## How the App Works

1. **Motion Detection**: Uses your device's accelerometer to detect shaking motion
2. **Sound Playback**: Plays a bell sound through your device's speakers
3. **Visual Feedback**: Shows color animations on the bell icon
4. **Settings**: Allows you to adjust sensitivity and threshold settings

All processing happens locally on your device. No data leaves your device.

## Data Storage

BikeBell does not store any persistent data. Settings are temporary and reset when the app is closed.

## Third-Party Services

BikeBell does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising.

## Children's Privacy

BikeBell does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes.

## Contact Information

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store listing.

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete.

## Compliance

This app complies with:
- Google Play Store privacy requirements
- Android privacy guidelines
- GDPR requirements (no data collection)

## Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there are no security concerns regarding data handling.
